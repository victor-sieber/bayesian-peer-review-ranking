# Re-identification risk of the granted proposals
A limited residual re-identification risk remains because the CRS has publicly
announced the four funded proposals and described the corresponding evaluation
rule. It may therefore be possible to identify their records in the anonymized
dataset and infer parts of their score profiles. This risk will be documented
transparently alongside the archived dataset.

A further residual risk arises from the unequal number of proposals assessed
by the nine reviewers. Individuals familiar with the review process may be
able to recognize a reviewer identifier from its number of assigned reviews
or from an unusual assignment pattern. Reviewer identities are therefore
replaced with random identifiers, and no names, affiliations, expertise
descriptions, or qualitative comments are included.
